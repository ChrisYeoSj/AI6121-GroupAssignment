HOW THIS K WAS CHOSEN
  Six chessboard shoots were taken. Candidates were compared by HELD-OUT
  cross-evaluation - each K evaluated against the other shoots' frames, with
  pose refit per image and K/dist frozen. This measures whether a calibration
  describes the CAMERA or merely fits its own data.

              on v1-set   on v2-set   on v3-set  |  HELD-OUT mean
    K_v1        1.743       2.087       2.357    |    2.222
    K_v2        1.967       1.906       2.187    |    2.077   <- selected
    K_v3        2.321       2.115       2.033    |    2.218

  v1 and v3 each win on their own frames then degrade by 0.5-0.6 px on unseen
  data - classic overfitting. v2 never wins its own set outright but is nearly
  flat across all three (spread 0.28 px vs v1's 0.61 px). v2 was selected.

  NOTE: in-sample RMS repeatedly misled during this work. A 1-image accidental
  fit scored 0.37 px with physically impossible parameters (fx=3352, fy=2831,
  k2=-41, k3=441). Always check held-out behaviour, not the headline RMS.

FOCAL LENGTH CONVERGENCE (the real evidence of correctness)
    Four independent solves:  fx = 3079 - 3115 px   spread 1.15%
    fx/fy = 1.008 in every solve
  Different days, different board placements, agreeing to ~1%.

WHY RMS 1.936 px IS NOT A PROBLEM
  Residuals were decomposed in BOARD coordinates (per corner index, averaged
  over all frames). If the error came from the lens model it would be tied to
  IMAGE position and would average out, since the board lands in a different
  place every frame. It does not average out - it stays locked to specific
  board corners.

    mean |residual|              1.69 px
    systematic component         1.09 px   <- repeats in every frame (64%)
    random component             0.70 px
    board scale ~15.5 px/mm  ->  1.09 px = 0.07 mm out-of-plane

  The RMS floor is 70 micrometres of paper unevenness in the printed board.
  Six reshoots confirmed this empirically: no shooting technique moves it.
  Only mounting the print on rigid flat stock (glass/acrylic) would.
  Eliminating it would leave ~0.7-0.9 px, under the 1.25 px threshold.

KNOWN LIMITATION - DISTORTION BEYOND ~74% RADIUS
  No calibration frame ever placed a corner beyond 74% of the corner radius.
  This is a GEOMETRIC CEILING of the board, not a technique failure: inner
  corners are inset one full square from the board edge, so with a 9x6 board
  filling the frame the outermost inner corner reaches at most ~77%.
  Three independent shoots (yours and a third party's) all landed 69-75%.

  Radial displacement in px, four independent solves:

    %radius   px-radius |   v1     v2     v3     v5  |  spread
       25%       630 px |   5.2    4.4    4.7    4.3 |    1.0 px
       50%      1260 px |  17.6   17.2   18.9   16.7 |    2.3 px
       74%      1865 px |  30.4   25.0   34.1   24.2 |    9.9 px  <- data ends
       85%      2142 px |  82.1   54.3   75.5   53.4 |   28.8 px  EXTRAPOLATED
      100%      2520 px | 320.5  175.5  236.0  174.2 |  146.4 px  EXTRAPOLATED

  Inside the data the four solves agree to 1-10 px. At the corner they disagree
  by 146 px, so any answer out there is arbitrary.

  This shows up as k2=-0.950 and k3=+1.368 being large and opposing: at the
  corner they contribute -0.438 and +0.429, nearly cancelling. That near-
  cancellation is a SYMPTOM of k2/k3 being correlated and individually ill-
  determined (k2 varies 39% and k3 53% across solves, while k1 varies 22%).
  It is also accidental to v2 - v1 nets +0.0707, not cancelling at all.

  DO NOT "fix" this by dropping k3. Held-out cross-validation:
      k1,k2,k3  held-out 1.822 px   <- best
      k1,k2     held-out 2.181 px
      k1 only   held-out 2.597 px
  k2/k3 do real work where observations exist; they are only untrustworthy
  where they do not.

  Scene feature distribution (3 frames, 12003 SIFT keypoints):
      median feature radius            66% of corner radius
      within 74% (validated region)    73.1%
      beyond 74% (extrapolated)        26.9%
      beyond 85%                        6.4%

  MITIGATION for Stage 3+: discard features beyond ~85% of corner radius after
  undistortion. Costs ~6% of keypoints, removes the least trustworthy ones.
  RANSAC will absorb most of the 74-85% band as outliers regardless.

4. NOTES FOR WHOEVER BUILDS STAGE 2+
================================================================================

  1. K IS VALID ONLY AT 3024x4032. fx scales with image width. Any resize
     silently invalidates it - the pipeline will still run and produce
     plausible-looking output. Process at native resolution. If a rescale is
     ever unavoidable, load_calib() in
     scripts/scene_quality_check/check_photos.py (lines 699-728) has the
     correct transform including the +0.5/-0.5 pixel-centre convention, and the
     rescaled K must be written back as THE K. There is only ever one.

  2. DISCARD FEATURES BEYOND ~85% OF CORNER RADIUS after undistortion.
     See section 1. Costs ~6% of keypoints.

  3. K IS NOT NEEDED FOR STAGE 2. Feature extraction (RootSIFT) is K-free.
     K first matters at Stage 3, fitting the essential matrix on rays K^-1 x.

  4. COLMAP SELF-CALIBRATED - this reference does NOT use your K. That is
     deliberate (independent cross-check), but means the comparison validates
     the calibration rather than depending on it.

  5. COLMAP DOES NOT APPLY EXIF ORIENTATION. It works on raw 4032x3024
     landscape pixels; our calibration and check_photos.py work on 3024x4032
     portrait (cv2 applies the rotation). Focal length in pixels is rotation-
     invariant so f stays comparable, but cx/cy will not line up.
